# Privacy Policy

Layers doesn't collect, store, or send any personal data.

## What It Accesses

- **Your HID Remapper.** Layers talks to a connected HID Remapper over USB to read which layer is active. It writes one expression into the device's RAM so it can read the layer back. Nothing is written to the device's flash, and unplugging it clears the change.
- **Its own settings.** Your choices (Show HUD, muted layers, HUD Show Duration, Open HUD on, Run on Startup) are saved on your PC under `HKCU\Software\Layers`.

## What It Doesn't Do

- No internet connection. Layers never sends anything over the network.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or ads.
- No accounts, and no access to your files, keystrokes, or other devices.
