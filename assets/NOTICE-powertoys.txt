The shortcut picker and its key caps are ported from microsoft/PowerToys:

  src/Layers.UI/Controls/KeyVisual.cs
  src/Layers.UI/Controls/KeyCharPresenter.cs
  src/Layers.UI/Styles/KeyVisual.xaml
  src/Layers.UI/Styles/KeyCharPresenter.xaml
  src/Layers.UI/Controls/ShortcutControl.xaml and .xaml.cs
  src/Layers.UI/Controls/ShortcutDialogContentControl.xaml and .xaml.cs
  src/Layers.UI/Interop/KeyboardHook.cs
  src/Layers.Core/Logic/HotkeySettings.cs

They are used by the shortcut sheet flyout and the Settings window's
Shortcuts page.

The usage name tables in src/Layers.Core/Logic/UsageNames.Tables.cs are
generated from config-tool-web/usages.js in jfedor2/hid-remapper, so the
sheet shows the same key names as the web config tool.
